## Libet Experiment (draft)

The brain “presses the button” before you believe you have made the decision.

In the Libet experiment, it was shown that the brain’s motor system begins preparing a movement before a person becomes consciously aware that they have decided to execute that movement immediately. The time gap is about 0.35 seconds, and the actual movement then occurs roughly another 0.2 seconds later.
