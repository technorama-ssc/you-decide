## Background

The Prisoner’s Dilemma shows just how difficult decisions can become when personal interest and the common good come into conflict. What seems rational for one person can lead to a worse outcome for everyone. This paradox is what makes the dilemma so fascinating — and invites us to look at many everyday decisions and conflicts with others from a different perspective.
